# WetinNext submission presentation

## Opening — 30 seconds

“I can find a hundred courses online, but which one should I start, what can I do with it, and what should I do next? WetinNext turns a learner's goal, location and constraints into a sourced starting guide, a practical learning plan and evidence of progress.”

Primary track: Guidance, Pathways & Opportunity. Supporting tracks: Access & Discovery and Engagement. The project owner originated the concept; Codex supported implementation and verification.

## Four grading criteria

| Criterion | Claim | Evidence to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uniqueness | Goal → sourced discovery → practical work → evidence → next application forms one continuous journey | Same goal shown in Discover, weekly review, skills and CV comparison |
| Scalability across geographies | Country scope and provider/source handling are separate from the learner journey | Nigeria/Rwanda/Global selection, gateway/service boundaries, SCALABILITY.md; no claim of load-tested scale |

## Questions to rehearse

- Why return? The next weekly action, saved work, practice feedback, goal history and application preparation continue the journey. Reminder settings exist; Telegram delivery is future work.
- Why not just ChatGPT? WetinNext adds persistent goals, structured sourced guides, a saved practical plan and evidence-linked review. It does not claim a smarter base model.
- Is four weeks enough? It is a starter, with extensions and additional learning branches. Professional qualifications and career readiness take longer.
- Are jobs guaranteed active? Listings require source and date evidence; deadlines are checked. The provider's application page is authoritative and must be checked before applying.
- Is eligibility automatic? The comparison identifies listed requirements and evidence gaps; it does not verify nationality, credentials or hiring suitability.
- Does it work offline? Saved guides persist on the server and text-first choices help reduce heavy media. Full offline operation and measured data savings are not implemented.
